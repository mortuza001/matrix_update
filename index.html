Firmware & Recovery

Infomatrix Firmware Update Guide

Upgrade your display over Wi-Fi (OTA) or perform a direct USB manual flash, and reach out if you need support.

Option 1: OTA Update Option 2: Manual Flash Support
Important: After a firmware update, your previous settings may be reset. You will need to reconfigure your device settings (Wi-Fi, preferences, license, etc.) before use.
Generate or retrieve your license key at: license.infomatrix3d.com

Option 1: OTA Update

For firmware version 1.9+, you can update directly over Wi-Fi:

  1. Download the latest firmware files from HERE.
  2. Visit the device update page at http://IP-address/update (for example, http://192.168.0.10/update):
    User: admin
    Pass: Device-ID
  3. Choose OTA Mode: Firmware → click Select file and choose the downloaded firmware.bin → start the update.
  4. Choose OTA Mode: LittleFS → click Select file and choose the downloaded filesystem.bin → start the update.

Option 2: Manual Flash

Prerequisites

  • A PC or laptop with a USB port.
  • A standard USB data cable.
  • An appropriate screwdriver.

Step 1: Access the Hardware

  1. Locate the side of the device cover that displays the “Infomatrix” label.
  2. Using a screwdriver, carefully remove the screws securing that one side of the cover (usually the right side when facing the back).
  3. Remove the cover to expose the internal components.
  4. Locate the microcontroller’s USB port (COM) inside the device.
  1. Disconnect the power cable and connect the controller to your PC using the USB data cable.

Step 2: Prepare to Flash — Firmware Upgrade

  1. Open a web browser on your PC (Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge).
  2. Navigate to Infomatrix Web Flash.
  3. Select your Infomatrix device model and desired version from the dropdown menu.
  4. Click the “Flash” button.
  5. A system pop-up window will appear asking you to select a connection port.
  6. Identify the correct COM Port associated with your connected device.
  7. Select the port and click “Connect” and “Install” to begin flashing.

Step 3: Completion

  1. Wait for the installation process to complete (typically 1–2 minutes).
  2. Once finished, your device will automatically reboot.
  3. Unplug the USB cable and reinstall the cover, ensuring the screws are not overtightened.
